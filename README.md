Qaphela
Cybersecurity, Privacy & Digital Trust

Qaphela is a mobile app project focused on helping people in South
Africa recognise potential scams, check suspicious links, and make safer
decisions online. "Qaphela" means to be alert or careful.
Project Overview
Scams and phishing attempts often arrive through SMS messages, links,
and online communications. Qaphela aims to make digital-safety
information clear and accessible, including for people with limited
technical knowledge.

The app's visual direction uses a burgundy, pink, and off-white
colour palette.
Key Goals
- Help users recognise common warning signs in suspicious messages.
- Provide guidance for assessing potentially unsafe links.
- Explain potential risks in clear, practical language.
- Improve cybersecurity awareness through educational content.
- Encourage responsible reporting of suspicious activity.
- Support accessible and multilingual safety guidance where available.
Features
The following are intended project features. Update this list to reflect
what is implemented and tested in the current version.
- SMS scam detection: Review message text for common scam
  indicators.
- Suspicious-link checking: Help users assess URLs for warning
  signs.
- Scan results: Explain potential risks and recommend safer next
  steps.
- Scam awareness: Provide educational information about phishing,
  fake job offers, fraudulent messages, and other common scams.
- Multilingual guidance: Present safety information in supported
  languages.
- Community reporting and blocklist: Support sharing information
  about suspicious links or numbers, subject to appropriate privacy
  and moderation safeguards.
- Accessible interface: Aim for clear navigation, readable text,
  and straightforward explanations.

Privacy and Security
Qaphela is intended to support cybersecurity awareness; it should not be
treated as a guarantee that a message, link, or phone number is safe.
- Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets
  in the repository.
- Do not upload real users' private messages or personal information
  without a lawful basis and appropriate consent.
- Clearly explain what data the app collects, why it is needed, and
  how it is handled.
- Treat automated scam assessments as indicators, not definitive
  proof.
- Verify any external service integrations and their permissions
  before release.
